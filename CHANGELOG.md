# Changelog

Every check-in to `main` bumps the version in `package.json` and adds an entry here (see "Versioning" in `CLAUDE.md`). The app shows this version, plus the build's commit and date, under **Profile → About**.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-30

- feat: show the app version, build commit and release date under Profile → About, with a "Check for updates" button.
- fix: the service worker now fetches pages network-first, so refreshing the app loads the latest deploy instead of a stale cached copy.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-29

- feat: curriculum grows from 11 to 32 stages across 8 worlds (ages 3–15), adding Fraction Falls and Algebra Summit.
- feat: levels are generated by difficulty, ramp from easy to hard within a session, adapt to the learner, and include method hints.
- feat: redesigned home mission card, practice screen (hints, retry, test-only hearts), results, journey trail, and 6-option placement.
- fix: returning guests can continue instead of re-onboarding and losing their stage.
